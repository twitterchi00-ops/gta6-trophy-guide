      About this guide

      Every trophy name, description and grade here is the official PlayStation trophy list, matched to the bounty kit. The guides are original — written from those official descriptions. Where a description is cryptic, or a trigger is not yet confirmed in-game, the guide says so instead of inventing a step.

      The four provisional missables

      Four trophies are flagged provisional missable: they may only be possible inside a story window, so the guide says to do them the first time the chance appears. They are Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Actions Speak Louder and Bad Luck, I Guess. “Provisional” means the flag comes from the trophy descriptions, not from a confirmed in-game test — if a later test shows one is repeatable in free roam, the flag should be removed.

      Sources

      • PlayStation official trophy descriptions (every name, description and grade).
      • PSNProfiles trophy listings — for the counted challenges (shooting range, drone races, meditation spots, cards, WAiNK, areas) and the community-confirmed triggers.
      • In-game menus — the stats, activities, challenges, collection, vehicle, property, business and gang-activity menus, which show the counts the game itself tracks.

      How progress is stored

      Ticking a trophy stores it in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use Reset progress on the Checklist to start over.

      What this guide does not do

      It does not show a map, and it does not track anything for you in-game. It is a checklist and a reading guide: the order (Roadmap), the trigger (each guide), and the warning (the missables).
